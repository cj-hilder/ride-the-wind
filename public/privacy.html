Privacy Policy

Ride the Wind · Effective 25 November 2026

Ride the Wind is a wind-aware bike commute app made by Chris Hilder. This policy explains what data the app handles and where that data goes. In short: your data stays on your phone, unless you choose to back it up to your own Google Drive. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and nothing is sold or shared.

Data stored on your device

Everything you create in Ride the Wind is stored only in your browser's storage on your device. That includes:

  • your routes, including the GPS track of any route you record
  • your rides: start and finish times, and the GPS track recorded while riding
  • each route's tuning, and your settings such as cruising speed and units
  • saved weather forecasts, kept so the app works offline

None of this is sent to us. You can delete all of it at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Location

The app uses your phone's GPS only while you record a route or a ride, and only after you allow it. Your location is stored on your device as part of that route or ride, and is not sent to us.

Weather forecasts

Forecasts come from Open-Meteo. To get them, the app sends Open-Meteo the coordinates of a few points along each of your routes (and, while you record a new route, of where you are). Open-Meteo receives these with your IP address, as any web service does. No account, name or other identifier is sent. Open-Meteo's own terms and privacy policy apply to those requests.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you connect a Google account, Ride the Wind backs up your app data to that account's Google Drive.

  • What the app can access. The app requests only the drive.appdata permission. This gives it access to a hidden folder in your Drive that belongs to Ride the Wind alone. The app cannot see, read or change any of your other Drive files.
  • What is stored there. Backup files containing your routes, rides, route tuning and settings, as listed above.
  • Your Google account email address is read so that the app can show which account is connected. It is kept only on your device.
  • Sign-in tokens. After you connect, Google issues a token that lets the app back up without asking you again. That token is stored on your device. When the app needs fresh access, it sends the token to Ride the Wind's own small server endpoint, which adds the app's Google credentials and passes the request to Google. That endpoint stores nothing and does not log tokens.
  • Disconnecting. Disconnect in the app revokes its access to your Google account. You can also revoke it at any time from your Google Account's security settings, under third-party connections. Your existing backups stay in your Drive until you delete them.
  • Deleting backups. In Google Drive on the web, open Settings, then Manage apps, find Ride the Wind, and choose "Delete hidden app data".

Ride the Wind's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Google account is used only to back up and restore your own Ride the Wind data. It is never used for advertising, never sold, never transferred to anyone else, and never read by a person.

Hosting

The app is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes your IP address and basic request details to deliver the app and protect the service, and may keep short-lived request logs. Ride the Wind does not use these logs to identify or track you.

Children

Ride the Wind is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

Under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020 you can ask for access to, or correction of, personal information held about you. Ride the Wind holds none outside your own device and your own Google Drive, both of which you control directly.
